# Home

The first screen: a director's desk, not a file list. It answers "what do I record next?" before anything else.

- **Use** as the app's start screen on Windows (sidebar) and phones (bottom tab bar with a record button in the middle).
- **Consumer provides** the scripts, the recent takes, the chosen devices, and the handlers.
- **Hero: Record next.** One stage-dark panel, the screen's single hero:
  - the next script's opening lines, already marked, on the stage;
  - the mode tiles (Camera, Screen, Both);
  - live device checks as pills: the camera, the microphone with a moving level, and "Follows your voice" when Voice pace is on;
  - the record button, with one line saying what will happen, and a Practice button.
- **Scripts** are marked pages, not rows: a title in the display face, the first lines with their marker swipes and glyphs, a pencil note from the director, and dots for takes (amber when a Director's Cut is ready). Arabic pages are right to left.
- **New script** is the last page, dashed: "Write, paste, or start from a sample".
- **Recent takes** are 16:9 thumbnails with the duration, and a "Cut ready" badge in `cue` amber.
- **Arrival:** the desk sets itself on load (sections rise in order), then the director's pass draws the marks on the pages.
- **Phone:** one column, the hero first, the tab bar at the bottom with the record button raised in its centre.
- **Don't** add a second hero, stats dashboards or promotional banners. **Don't** show an empty page: a new user sees the three sample scripts, already marked up.
